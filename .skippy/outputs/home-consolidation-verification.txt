Home consolidation implementation

Home now contains Today, This Week, and collapsed-by-default All. The three inventories use the same row and action controls. Add task/want/repeat controls and area filtering (including Unsorted and All areas) remain available. All adds Completed/cancelled and Paused/retired filters and calendar horizon continuation.

Removed the Agenda navigation destination and its Home link. /tasks performs a client-side redirect preserving search and hash. Home resolves task, event, and recurrence hashes with an owned exact-record query, including completed and project-linked records. Missing records display an unavailable state.

New inventory reads use bounded indexed seeks and source continuations. All displays 50 rows at a time; periods display at most 20. Filtered source batches can be short. Automatic scanning stops after ten batches and offers continuation instead of unbounded background work. Historical event starts are scanned in bounded batches to preserve ongoing events that began before the lookback; very large calendar histories can therefore need continuation before current events appear. Distinct canonical records remain separate; repeat claims are resolved against owned live tasks before filtering.
