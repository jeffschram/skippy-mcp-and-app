Implemented phase action controls

Home rows and the item detail modal share completion controls and per-item pending/error state with synchronous submission guards. Secondary actions live in details. Task forms persist title/description/deadline edits, including ordinary deadline clearing; support an owned waiting-person picker and clearing waiting; preserve draft-only nudge approval; and link the same task to an existing project with an explanation that it leaves all personal Home sections.

Event details show stored calendar/conference HTTP(S) links, an encoded Maps location link, and description. Invalid or missing links are hidden. Events have no completion control. No calendar writes were added.

Repeat details support plain-language cadence changes, anchor/timezone/explicit next-due edits, completion, skip, pause/resume, and recent history. The occurrence lifecycle atomically preserves task identity, closes the live task, updates the schedule/version, and stores a durable request outcome. Replaying a request returns its original result; another stale request cannot advance the occurrence. Skip records a separate outcome, leaves lastCompletedAt unchanged, cancels the live task, and advances from the scheduled cadence. Edits update a live task's deadline without replacing its identity or description. Pause/resume preserves dates/history and leaves the task open. History remains capped at 24 entries, including legacy completion records.

Home, Mind, knowledge task completion, project-linked task execution, and MCP repeat completion use the protected lifecycle. Completion retains attention/execution/dependency updates. Waiting targets and linked records are owner checked. Wants cannot receive deadlines; blank titles and invalid dates are rejected. Old interval schedules now advance beyond the former 400-day ceiling; multi-period calendar schedules retain their original cadence across missed periods.
