# Mind: Bauhaus experiment

Local browser-rendering experiment on `feature/bauhaus-experiment`. Stored records, attention and relationships are unchanged.

## Composition

Categories retain their colors and become shallow whole forms. Selected records anchor one of three composition patterns: stepped structure for projects/tasks, overlapping diagonals for people, or offset columns. Importance chooses apparent radii of 2.3, 3.5, 5 or 6.8 for 0–1, 2–4, 5–9 or 10+ saved neighbors. The owner's radius is capped at 3.8 so it cannot overwhelm other collections.

Depth now ranges from 0 to 6, with physical size compensating to maintain the intended apparent hierarchy. Pointer tilt is limited to approximately a quarter degree. Reduced-motion preferences disable tilt and transitions. Drag pans, scroll zooms.

The overview places individual records on one shared picture plane in stable importance/ID order. Up to three large forms establish a broad diagonal; remaining records fill the same gaps using a score for compactness, exposed silhouettes, nearby edges, and horizontal/vertical/diagonal alignment. Saved relationships add a soft attraction, while unconnected records participate in the same composition. There are no neighborhood bounding boxes or separate band for singles. Proximity does not create a saved relationship. Selected compositions retain their separate motif rules. Focusing or searching composes the relevant subset while noninteractive background shapes fade to 0.6% opacity.

Limited overlaps preserve exposed areas; translucency creates pigment mixing. A shared smooth pigment texture and subtle screen-space grain avoid magnified speckling. Selection uses a single front-contour outline and a quiet title label; immediate attention retains its coral outline.

Connection lines are hidden in the overview. Focus shows up to five links for small collections or three for larger ones; hovering a record reveals its exact links. Lines route along shared horizontal/vertical segments with endpoints trimmed to shape boundaries. Overlapping forms omit the redundant wire. All lines correspond to real saved relationships.

## Validation and remaining scope

Tests cover shallow depth and importance, the owner size cap, stable ordering/duplicate links/status refresh, selected anchors, saved versus synthetic links, empty/singleton focus, and boundary routing. Existing map/completion tests remain. Browser checks cover overview and project focus without changing user records.

This remains an experimental algorithmic composition. Dense data and unusually shaped records can need further art direction; List remains available for direct access to every sampled record. No new dependencies or Convex sync are required.
